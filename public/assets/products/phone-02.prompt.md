# iPhone product background edit

Tool: built-in imagegen (edit), followed by WebP encoding at quality 0.92.
Input and final project asset: `public/assets/products/phone-02.webp`.
The input was the product image supplied by the project owner.

## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied phone product photograph.
Replace only the warm gray/beige background with a clean pure white (#FFFFFF)
background suitable for an e-commerce product card. Preserve the exact existing
phone, its off-white color, cameras, logo, metallic edges, proportions, centered
position, size and rear-view orientation. Preserve the full phone with existing
margins and portrait framing. Keep only a subtle soft neutral contact shadow
immediately beneath the phone. No gray or beige background, no added objects,
no text, no watermark. Do not redesign or recolor the phone.
