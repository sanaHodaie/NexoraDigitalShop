# White backgrounds for extra product photos

Mode: built-in imagegen edit, 2026-10-17. Existing uploaded photographs are the edit targets.

Saved assets: `extra-03.webp` (monitor), `extra-05.webp` (projector),
`extra-06.webp` (microphone; product ID extra-07), `extra-08.webp` (reader),
`extra-09.webp` (pen tablet and accessories).

Same prompt used separately for each source:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the provided ecommerce product photograph. Change ONLY the off-white/gray background to perfectly uniform pure white #FFFFFF, including all corners and empty space between objects. Preserve the exact original product(s), accessories, packaging, screen content, branding, text, geometry, colors, lighting on the object, position, scale, and camera angle. Retain all objects shown; do not remove the box, pen, cable, stand or rear view if present. Keep the original landscape framing and aspect ratio. No redesign, no invented details, no cropping, no new text or watermark. Minimize floor shadow to a faint neutral contact shadow directly beneath the product; no gradient or gray vignette. Output one edited photo, opaque white background.

Generated images were visually reviewed, then re-encoded as WebP at width 1000px and quality 0.85 for the storefront. Product image URLs are unchanged.
