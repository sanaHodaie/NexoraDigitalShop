# Accessory background removal

Edited with the built-in imagegen tool. Final assets:
`accessory-02.webp`, `accessory-07.webp`, `accessory-08.webp`, `accessory-09.webp`.
The generated PNGs were converted to WebP at 1000px width, preserving alpha.

The following prompt was used separately with each original product photograph:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit the supplied ecommerce product photograph only: remove the entire pale gray/white background and its background shadow, replacing it with genuine transparent alpha, including empty spaces inside cable loops and between connectors. Preserve the exact photographed product, all parts, proportions, viewpoint, position, original colors, logos and text without redesign or extra objects. Keep the original landscape canvas and product size, crisp natural edges, no white fringe, no checkerboard painted into pixels. Output one transparent PNG cutout.
